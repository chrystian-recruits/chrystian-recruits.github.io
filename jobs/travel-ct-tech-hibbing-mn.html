About this assignment

Medical Solutions is hiring a CT / X-Ray Technologist for a travel contract at an acute care hospital in Hibbing, Minnesota. This is a direct-partnership opening — you would be working this job with Medical Solutions, with no middle-vendor layer on the submission.

You will perform CT and X-Ray imaging, including code strokes and traumas, and start IVs. The department uses Siemens equipment (the CT is called a Flash) and Epic for charting. Volume is typically about 20–30 CTs per day per room, with X-Ray around 20 exams per day. Scrubs can be any color or print.

Orientation is one day of online learning modules, two days in X-Ray, and two days in CT. Travelers rarely work solo.

Schedule

  • Five 8-hour shifts, 40 hours guaranteed each week
  • Typical hours: 12:30 p.m.–8:30 p.m., but the shift can be day, evening, or night
  • Shift lengths and start times can vary; weekend shifts as needed
  • No on-call requirement
  • Cancellation policy: up to 3 shifts may be cancelled on a 13-week assignment (4 shifts on 14–20 weeks, 5 shifts on 21+ weeks)

Estimated pay

  • Estimated weekly gross (taxable wages + stipends): $3,665.34–$3,807.16
  • Estimated taxable weekly wages: $1,950.34–$2,092.16 ($48.76–$52.30/hour)
  • Estimated weekly stipends: $1,715.00 (if you qualify)
  • Overtime wage: $73.14–$78.46/hour

Pay is an estimate of weekly gross, not a guarantee of take-home pay. The package may combine taxable hourly wages with tax-free housing and meals stipends if you qualify. Final pay depends on your tax home, hours worked, overtime, and contract details, which your recruiter will confirm before you sign.

Requirements

  • At least 1 year of experience, with prior training in CT and X-Ray
  • IV experience required
  • Experience with code strokes and traumas required
  • Graduate of an approved AMA school of Radiologic Technology
  • Registered with the ARRT
  • BLS required
  • License required at submission
  • XR Registry and CT certification are beneficial; strong CT experience may be considered
  • First-time travelers are welcome
